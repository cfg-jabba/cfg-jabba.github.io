Short clips from your streams, posted on your schedule.

Clipper Studio finds the best moments in a creator’s livestreams and gameplay, edits them into captioned vertical clips, and publishes the ones the creator approves to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Who uses it

Streamers and game studios in the Clipper creator program. Each creator connects their own accounts and only ever publishes to those accounts. Joining is by invitation through the program; there is no open sign-up.

How it works

  1. Clips are made. Clipper Studio picks moments from the creator’s streams and renders 9:16 clips with captions.
  2. The creator connects TikTok. They sign in with TikTok and allow Clipper Studio to post to their account. They can remove that access at any time from TikTok’s settings.
  3. The creator reviews every clip. On the Publisher page they watch each clip, edit its caption, and choose who can view it, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and whether it promotes a brand. Nothing is preselected.
  4. The creator approves it. They confirm they want it posted, either now or at a time they pick.
  5. Clipper Studio posts it. At that time the clip is published to the creator’s TikTok with exactly the settings they chose.

What creators control

  • Which clips are posted, and when.
  • Every caption and every privacy and interaction setting.
  • Branded-content disclosure, following TikTok’s rules.
  • Cancelling a scheduled post before it goes out.
  • Disconnecting their account at any time.

What it never does

  • Post anything a creator has not approved.
  • Read followers, messages, comments or viewing history.
  • Sell or share creator data.
