Privacy Policy — Clipper Studio Co

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Clipper Studio Co (“the app”) is a personal desktop tool used by its sole operator to publish his own short-form videos to his own social accounts. It has no public sign-up, no other users, and no server.

What the app accesses

When a TikTok account is connected, the app receives an access token that lets it upload videos to that account only. It requests no other permission.

What the app stores

  • The access token, in a file on the operator’s own computer. It is never transmitted anywhere except to TikTok’s own API when publishing.
  • Video files and their titles, on the operator’s own computer.

That is the complete list. The app runs locally and has no database, no analytics, no cloud storage, and no third-party services.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read, collect, or store any other person’s data.
  • It does not read followers, messages, comments, or viewing history.
  • It does not sell, share, or transmit data to anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising, profiling, or training.
  • It does not act on behalf of anyone other than its operator.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and video files live on the operator’s computer for as long as he keeps them and can be deleted at any time by deleting the files. Revoking the app’s access in TikTok’s own settings immediately and permanently ends its ability to publish.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
