Privacy Policy — Clipper Studio Co

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Clipper Studio Co (“Clipper Studio”) edits creators’ stream highlights into short clips and publishes the clips a creator approves to that creator’s own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is used by creators in the Clipper creator program, who join by invitation.

What we access

When a creator connects TikTok, TikTok gives Clipper Studio an access token with only the permissions the creator approved on TikTok’s consent screen:

  • Basic profile info (display name, avatar, open ID), shown on the Publisher page so the creator can see which account a clip will post to.
  • Posting: uploading clips to that creator’s account, as a draft or as a post with the settings the creator chose.
  • Posting settings TikTok reports for the account (allowed privacy options, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are available, maximum video length), read each time the Publisher page is opened.

We request nothing else. We never read followers, messages, comments, likes or viewing history.

What we store

  • Access and refresh tokens, in a database on Clipper Studio’s own computer. They are sent only to TikTok’s API and are never logged, shared or published.
  • The clips, their captions, and the settings the creator chose for each one (privacy, interactions, disclosure, scheduled time), plus the time the creator approved it.
  • The publish ID and status TikTok returns for each post, so a clip is never posted twice.

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking services, and we do not sell or share any data with anyone.

How we use it

Only to show the creator their account on the Publisher page and to post the clips they approved, when they asked for them to be posted. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or training models.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account stays connected. A creator can disconnect at any time in TikTok’s settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), which immediately ends our ability to post; on request we delete their tokens, clips and posting history within 30 days. Scheduled posts can be cancelled on the Publisher page before they go out.

Children

Clipper Studio is not directed at children under 18 and is not used to collect anyone’s personal data.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
