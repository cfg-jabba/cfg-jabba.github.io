What Clipper Studio is

Clipper Studio Co (“Clipper Studio”) edits highlights from a creator’s livestreams and gameplay into short vertical clips and publishes the clips the creator approves to the creator’s own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Who may use it

Creators in the Clipper creator program, who join by invitation. Each creator connects only accounts they own or are authorised to manage, and Clipper Studio posts only to the account the creator connected.

Creator approval

Nothing is posted without the creator’s approval. For every clip the creator reviews the video, edits the caption, chooses the privacy level and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, declares any branded or promotional content, and confirms they want it posted, either immediately or at a time they choose. A scheduled clip can be cancelled until it is posted.

Content

Creators are responsible for the clips they approve and must hold the rights to publish them. Posts must follow TikTok’s Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Branded Content Policy, and creators agree to TikTok’s Music Usage Confirmation when they post.

Platform access

Clipper Studio posts through TikTok’s official Content Posting API using access the creator granted on TikTok’s own consent screen. It does not scrape, automate the TikTok app or website, circumvent rate limits, or access anything beyond the permissions granted. Creators can revoke access at any time in TikTok’s settings, which immediately ends Clipper Studio’s ability to post.

Clipper Studio is provided as-is, without warranty. Platforms may delay, limit or reject posts, and we are not liable for a platform’s decisions.
