# Supplier sourcing & vetting

This is the highest-leverage week of the whole project. A weak magnet turns
every sale into a refund, and no amount of good marketing survives that.

**Rule: order samples from three suppliers. Always three. Never skip this.**

---

## 1. Where to look

I can't verify any supplier's current stock or terms for you — inventory and
policies change constantly, and anything I asserted here would be stale within
weeks. So this is where to search and what to demand, not a vetted list.

**Platforms with U.S.-stocked, blind-dropship jewelry suppliers:**

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Trendsi** | U.S.-based fashion/jewelry dropship, blind by default. Usually the best first stop for this category. |
| **Spocket** | Filter hard on "US" suppliers. Its whole pitch is domestic stock. |
| **Zendrop** | Has a U.S. warehouse program; confirm the specific SKU is in it, not just the account. |
| **Doba** | Older, broader catalog, more variable quality. |
| **Inventory Source** | Aggregator; useful if you want to compare several at once. |
| **Alibaba + a U.S. 3PL** | Best margin by far, but requires buying inventory. Revisit at month 3 if the product proves out — not now. |

**Search terms that actually work:** *magnetic clasp converter*, *magnetic
jewelry clasp closure*, *necklace clasp helper*, *magnetic clasp extender*,
*jewelry findings magnetic clasp*.

## 2. What to demand in writing before ordering

Email the supplier and get **written** answers. If they dodge any of these,
walk away — there are dozens of others.

1. **Is this SKU physically in a U.S. warehouse right now?** Which state?
2. **What is your blind-shipping policy?** Will the packing slip carry *my*
   brand and *no* reference to you or to China?
3. **What is your actual average ship time** to a U.S. residential address,
   measured from order placed to carrier scan?
4. **What magnet grade are these?** (You want N45 or better. N35 is weak.)
5. **What is the clasp body made of?** Stainless steel or brass. Reject zinc
   alloy — it discolors and turns green on skin.
6. **What is your defect/replacement policy** when a customer receives a broken unit?
7. **Do you have a per-unit price break**, and at what volume?

Keep these replies. If they later contradict themselves, you have the record.

## 3. The physical tests — run every one on every sample

Do these the day the samples land. Write the results in the table at the
bottom. Be ruthless; you are protecting yourself from a refund wave.

### Test 1 — Pull strength (the critical one)
Attach the converter to a chain. Hang the chain from something fixed. Hang a
weight from the bottom of the converter and increase it gradually.

- **Must hold 8 oz (225 g)** without separating. That's a chain plus an
  ordinary pendant plus a safety margin.
- **Good is 12 oz+.**
- **Under 6 oz: reject the supplier outright.** No exceptions, no matter how
  cheap. This is the failure mode that kills the business.

A kitchen scale works: press the converter down onto the scale until it
separates, and read the peak.

### Test 2 — Lateral shear
Magnetic clasps fail sideways long before they fail in tension. Grip both
halves and slide them across each other. If they slip apart under light
sideways pressure, the seating is bad — a necklace will shed it during normal
movement. Reject.

### Test 3 — One-handed, behind the neck, eyes closed
The entire product promise. Put on a necklace with the converter fitted,
using one hand, behind your head, without looking.

**If you cannot do this in under 5 seconds, the product does not work.** Have
someone with reduced hand dexterity try it too if you possibly can — you are
not the customer and your hands are probably better than hers.

### Test 4 — Finish and skin safety
- Rub the finish hard with a thumb for 30 seconds. Does plating come off?
- Tape one to your wrist for 24 hours. Any green mark or irritation = zinc
  alloy or nickel. **Reject** — this audience has sensitive skin and will say so
  publicly.

### Test 5 — The postmark test
Look at the package your sample arrived in. Where was it actually posted?
If a supplier claimed U.S. stock and the sample came from Shenzhen, they
lied about the one thing the whole business model depends on. Walk away.

### Test 6 — Repeated use
Open and close it 100 times. Check the jump ring hasn't opened, the plating
hasn't worn at the join, and the magnet hasn't weakened. Re-run Test 1.

## 5. After you choose

- [ ] Order a **second** sample from the winner. Consistency between batches is
      its own risk, and you want to know now rather than at order 50.
- [ ] Photograph the winning sample properly — those photos become the store's
      image slots. Natural light, a real hand, age-appropriate. See `AD-CREATIVE.md`.
- [ ] Ask about volume pricing at 100 and 500 units. Even if you don't buy yet,
      you'll know what month 3 looks like.
- [ ] Keep a rejected sample. When you eventually get a customer complaint about
      strength, you'll want the comparison.
