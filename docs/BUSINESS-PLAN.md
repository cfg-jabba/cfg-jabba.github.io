# Everly Grace — operating plan

**Brand:** faceless — no one appears on camera, ever
**Product:** magnetic clasp converters, 3-pack (gold / silver / rose gold)
**Price:** $29, free U.S. shipping
**Buyer:** women 50–65, U.S., moderate-to-good disposable income
**Model:** blind dropship from a U.S. warehouse. We never handle inventory.

---

## 1. Unit economics

Per order, at $29 with free shipping:

| Line | Amount | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Revenue | $29.00 | |
| Product cost (3 units) | −$6.00 | verify with samples; $4.50–7.50 is the realistic band |
| Supplier shipping charge | −$5.00 | U.S. blind dropship, padded mailer |
| Stripe (2.9% + $0.30) | −$1.14 | |
| **Contribution margin** | **$16.86** | |

**$16.86 is the break-even CPA.** Above that, every order loses money.

**Sensitivity — this is the number to watch:**

| Product+ship cost | Contribution | Break-even CPA |
|---|---:|---:|
| $9 (good sourcing) | $18.86 | $18.86 |
| $11 (expected) | $16.86 | $16.86 |
| $14 (poor sourcing) | $13.86 | $13.86 |

Every $1 saved on sourcing is $1 of CPA headroom. This is why the sample
round matters more than the ad round.

### Raising the ceiling
Contribution is the constraint, not traffic. Three levers, in order of effort:

1. **Order-bump at checkout** — a second set at $19 (Stripe supports this).
   Takes ~20% of buyers, adds ~$2.60 average contribution per order.
2. **6-pack at $44** — better $/order, same shipping cost. Push as "one for
   you, one for your sister."
3. **Second product** to the same list — this is where the real money is.
   See §5.

## 3. Why organic comes first

Break-even CPA is $16.86. A first-time advertiser with cold creative and no
pixel history realistically pays $20–40. So paid traffic on day one loses
money by construction.

Organic costs $0 and answers the only question that matters — *does the demo
make people want it?* If a Reel gets 40k views without spend, the message
works, and paid starts from a warm audience and proven creative at maybe
$12–18 CPA instead of $35.

**Gate:** do not spend a dollar on ads until one organic video clears
**10,000 views or 300 saves.** If nothing does after 20 posts, the product or
the angle is wrong, and $190 of ad spend would not have fixed it.

## 4. 30-day plan

### Week 1 — sourcing (the week that decides everything)
- [ ] Order samples from **3 different suppliers**. Never fewer than three.
- [ ] Register the .com. Point GitHub Pages at it.
- [ ] Set up Stripe, create the Payment Link, paste into `index.html`.
- [ ] Set up Formspree, Google Voice number, business email.
- [ ] Open a MailerLite account (free tier).

### Week 2 — proof
- [ ] **Run the magnet tests** in `SUPPLIER-VETTING.md`. Reject failures.
- [ ] Pick the winning supplier. Confirm blind shipping and U.S. stock **in writing.**
- [ ] Film 6–10 **faceless** videos — tabletop macro and the velvet bust, no
      person in frame. Four shot types and six scripts in `AD-CREATIVE.md`.
- [ ] Make 10 Pinterest pins from the same stills. Best-fit channel for a
      faceless brand and the one most people skip.
- [ ] Post daily to Reels, TikTok, Pinterest. Join 10 relevant Facebook groups
      and *participate* — do not post links yet, you will be removed.

### Week 3 — read the signal
- [ ] Keep posting daily. Track views/saves per video.
- [ ] **If a video clears the gate:** put $10/day behind that exact video for
      6 days. Target women 52–68, U.S.
- [ ] **If nothing clears it:** do not start ads. Reassess the angle. Try the
      gift angle and the bracelet angle, which are underused.
- [ ] Wire up the email flows in `EMAIL-FLOWS.md`.

### Week 4 — decide
- [ ] Compute actual CPA and actual contribution.
- [ ] Ask the go/kill question in §6.
- [ ] Email every buyer the day-14 review request. Real reviews are the
      single highest-leverage asset you can hold at this stage.

## 5. Where the money actually is

Month 1 is not profitable. It buys information. Plan for that.

| | Orders | Revenue | Contribution | Net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Month 1 (organic-first) | 3–20 | $87–580 | $50–340 | **−$70 to +$220** |
| Month 2 (proven creative) | 25–60 | $725–1,740 | $420–1,010 | −$100 to +$500 |
| Month 3 (+ email list) | 50–120 | $1,450–3,480 | $840–2,020 | +$200 to +$1,200 |

These are estimates with wide error bars, not forecasts. The honest read:
**this is a few-thousand-a-month business if it works.** It is a good first
product because it fails cheaply and teaches quickly — not because it is big.

The genuine asset being built is **the email list**: a few thousand U.S. women
aged 50–65 who have already bought an easy-fastening product from you. That
list is worth more than the clasp business, and it is what makes product two
(button hooks, magnetic-close bracelets, easy-grip garden tools, no-tie laces)
profitable from day one.

## 6. The go / kill decision (end of week 4)

**Continue if any one of these is true:**
- CPA under $20 on any audience, or
- an organic video over 25k views, or
- 15+ orders with zero refund requests.

**Kill if all of these are true:**
- 20+ organic posts, none over 5k views, and
- CPA above $35, and
- refund rate above 15%.

Killing at $250 spent is a *good outcome*. The failure mode to avoid is
spending $2,000 to avoid admitting the first $250 didn't work.

## 7. Risks, honestly

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Magnets too weak → refunds | **High** | The sample round exists for this. Reject anything under the pull test. |
| Supplier ships from China despite claiming U.S. stock | Medium | Order a sample to your own address and look at the postmark. |
| Meta rejects ads as "medical" | Medium | Never claim it treats arthritis. Say "easier to fasten." Copy in `AD-CREATIVE.md` is already compliant. |
| Product is easily copied | **Certain** | It is. The moat is the list and the brand, not the SKU. Act accordingly. |
| Pacemaker liability | Low but serious | Warning is on the FAQ and Terms. Keep it there. Do not remove it for conversion reasons. |
| Nobody cares | Medium | This is what week 3's gate measures. Cheap to find out. |
