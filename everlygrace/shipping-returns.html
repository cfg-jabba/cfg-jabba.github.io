Shipping & Returns

Last updated: [set this date when you publish]

Short version: we ship free from inside the United States, it usually takes three to six days, and if you don't like it we refund you and you keep it.

Where we ship from

Orders are packed and shipped from a fulfillment warehouse in the United States. We mention this because a lot of small online shops don't, and their parcels turn up three weeks later from overseas. Ours don't.

How long it takes

  • Processing: 1 business day. Orders placed on a weekend go out Monday.
  • Transit: 2–5 business days anywhere in the contiguous United States.
  • Alaska, Hawaii and U.S. territories: usually 5–9 business days.

You'll get an email with a tracking number as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. If that email hasn't arrived within two business days of your order, something has gone wrong — please tell us and we'll chase it.

What shipping costs

Nothing. Shipping is free on every order to a U.S. address, with no minimum. We don't currently ship internationally.

Returns — 60 days, and you keep the item

If your set isn't right for any reason, email us within 60 days of delivery and we will refund the full amount to your original payment method.

You do not need to send anything back, and you do not need to give a reason. This isn't generosity — postage on a $29 item costs more than the item is worth to us, and we would genuinely rather you kept it and thought well of us than went to the post office out of obligation.

Refunds are issued within two business days of your email. Your bank may take a further three to five days to show it.

If the magnets aren't strong enough for a particular necklace — which can happen with genuinely heavy statement pieces — write to us before requesting a refund. Using two converters, one on each side, solves it most of the time, and we'll send you a second set free rather than have you give up on it.

Damaged or wrong items

If your order turns up damaged, or it isn't what you ordered, send us a photo and we'll ship a replacement the same day. We won't ask you to return the damaged one.

Cancelling an order

If you need to cancel, email us as quickly as you can. If it hasn't been packed yet we'll cancel and refund immediately. If it's already gone out, the 60-day refund above covers you regardless.

Getting hold of us

Use the contact page. A real person reads every message, usually the same day, and always within one business day.
