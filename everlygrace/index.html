Questions people ask

Will it hold my necklace, or will it fall off?

The magnets are considerably stronger than they look — well beyond the weight of a standard chain or an ordinary pendant. For a genuinely heavy statement piece we'd suggest using two converters, one on each side. If yours doesn't hold, that's exactly what the 60-day return is for.

Does it work on bracelets too?

Yes, and honestly that's where people are most surprised — fastening a bracelet one-handed is close to impossible otherwise. The same converter works on bracelets, anklets and most watch chains.

Do I have to cut or remove my existing clasp?

No. Nothing is altered. The converter attaches to the clasp that's already there, and if you ever want to go back you just unclip it.

Is it safe with a pacemaker or other implanted device?

These contain small permanent magnets. Guidance from device manufacturers is generally to keep magnets at least six inches from an implanted pacemaker or defibrillator, and a necklace clasp sits closer than that. If you have any implanted medical device, please ask your doctor or your device manufacturer before wearing one. We're not able to advise on this ourselves, and we'd rather say so plainly than guess.

How long does delivery take?

We ship from a warehouse in the United States, so orders typically arrive in three to six business days. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves.

What if it isn't right for me?

Email us within 60 days and we'll refund you in full. You don't need to ship anything back and you don't need to explain why. Returning a $29 item costs more in postage than it's worth to us, and we'd rather you just keep it and think well of us.
