Everly Grace (“we”) sells jewelry accessories at [yourdomain.com]. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it. We've tried to write it in plain English.

What we collect

  • Order information. Your name, shipping address, email address and phone number, so we can send you what you bought and tell you where it is.
  • Payment information. Handled entirely by Stripe. Your card number never reaches our website and we never see or store it.
  • Email address, if you subscribe to our list or ask us a question.
  • Basic analytics. Pages visited and roughly where in the world the visit came from. We don't use this to identify you personally.

What we do with it

We use it to fulfil your order, answer your questions, and — if you've opted in — send you occasional email. That's the whole list.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that make the store work, and only with what they need:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Our fulfillment warehouse — your name and shipping address, to pack and post your order.
  • Our email provider — your email address, if you subscribed.
  • Formspree — the contents of a contact form, if you send one.
